## Week 1: Instructional Problem & Requirements Analysis

Answer and draft the following sections for your team's engineering portfolio (sample Table of Contents structure):

- **Instructional Problem** — What problem are you solving, and for whom?
- **Current State — Introductory KSAs** — What do learners/users know and do today?
- **Desired State — Post-Onboarding KSAs** — What should they know and do after your solution?
- **Gap Between Current and Desired States** — What's missing?
- **Instructional Expectations** — What outcomes should your solution guarantee?
- **Research Topic** — What are you studying, specifically?
- **Research Topic Importance** — Why does this matter?
- **Research Questions**
  - **RQ1 — Learner/User Experience**
  - **RQ2 — Support and Progress**
- **Research Purpose** — What will answering these questions accomplish?

### Team Norms & Roles
- Establish team norms and allocate individual technical ownership roles.
- Conduct an initial requirements-gathering interview with an industry mentor.

### Hardware Innovation
Consider whether adding a hardware component strengthens your solution (e.g. sensors, GPUs, embedded devices).
