## Week 2: Design Specifications & Ethics

### Problem Identification
Draft a formal problem statement backed by statistical data, market research, or direct observation from affected stakeholders.

### Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Author a PRD defining strict, quantifiable target metrics, for example:
- System latency < 200ms
- Weight < 5 lbs
- Accuracy > 90%

### Ethics & Intellectual Property
Conduct a patent and open-source license search (MIT, GPL, Apache) to ensure your proposed solution does not violate existing IP.

### Peer Review (closes Week 2)
Trade your problem statement, literature review, and PRD draft with another
team.

- Reviewers: check the problem statement is backed by evidence, the PRD
  metrics are quantifiable (not vague), and the literature review covers at
  least three prior-art sources with real limitations identified.
- Authors: log the feedback you received and note which points you'll revise
  before presenting to your mentor in Week 3.
