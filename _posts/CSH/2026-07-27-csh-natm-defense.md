## Week 12: Presentation of Designer's Recommendations

**PLTW Element L** — Presentation of Designer's Recommendations.

### Draft Recommendations for the Next Iteration
- Based on your Element I test data, Element J external evaluation, and Element K reflection, write concrete **Designer's Recommendations**: what should change in the Sprint 4 redesign, and why.
- Recommendations should be specific and evidence-backed (e.g. "switch to server-side validation — 40% of external evaluators triggered the client-side bug") — not vague ("make it better").

### Finalize the Electronic Portfolio
- Your **Electronic Portfolio** is the full, navigable record of Sprints 1-3: problem statement, literature review, PRD, decision matrix, BOM, build procedure, test data, mentor logs, external evaluation, and this week's recommendations.
- No paper. Everything lives in your GitHub repo / portfolio site, linked and versioned.

### Night at the Museum (N@tM)
**N@tM** is your Alpha prototype defense — a showcase where your team presents like a museum exhibit to an evaluation panel (mentors, teachers, and/or outside guests) who walk from team to team.
- Prepare a **Multimedia Display**: your prototype running live if possible, plus a visual summary (poster, slides, or screen loop) of problem → process → results → recommendations.
- Be ready to explain and defend your design decisions to someone seeing your project for the first time — this is the same muscle as the mentor and external evaluation meetings, at higher stakes.

### Core Deliverables
- Designer's Recommendations for Sprint 4.
- Finalized electronic portfolio (Sprints 1-3).
- N@tM multimedia display + live defense of the Alpha prototype.
